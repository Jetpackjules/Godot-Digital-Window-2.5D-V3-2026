# Original Gothic window reference prompt

Create a reconstruction-friendly photorealistic reference image for a 2.5D
architectural asset. A monumental medieval Gothic stone window wall seen from
the interior, straight-on at human eye height, near-orthographic perspective,
filling an exact widescreen 16:9 frame edge to edge with almost no empty wall.
Use one broad central Gothic arcade composed of three closely spaced pointed
lancet openings and restrained authentic tracery, not three repeated pasted
window modules. The openings occupy roughly 70 percent of the image and are
pure matte black empty holes with absolutely no glass, no scenery, no sky, and
no reflections. Show substantial real depth: thick weathered limestone jambs,
deep reveals, a heavy sill, a shallow soffit, chipped edges, irregular medieval
masonry, subtle mortar, and believable photogrammetry-like surface variation.
Dim neutral interior ambience with soft grazing side light only to reveal
geometric depth; avoid theatrical orange-and-blue lighting. Front-facing
composition suitable for depth estimation and mesh reconstruction, sharp
material detail, coherent single stone type across frame. No people, furniture,
candles, rain, fog, text, logos, watermark, border, or UI.
