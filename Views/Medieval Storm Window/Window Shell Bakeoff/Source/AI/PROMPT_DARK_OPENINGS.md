# Dark openings alternative prompt

Create an alternate, front-on medieval-window façade for the 2.5D bakeoff.
Three very tall pointed Gothic apertures must span roughly 70% of the frame.
Use nearly black, subtly rim-lit interior-facing stone for the columns, arches,
and lintel. Keep side jambs and sill narrow. The three apertures are uniform
pure black, with no scenery, glass, rain, reflections, haze, or shadow. Avoid
bright exterior wall, orange light, extra openings, furniture, text, logos, and
watermarks.
